Here is my final project. My project is a device that simplifies the process of selecting a wax type for a given race day. Ski wax changes drastically based on the tempature. The on board RGBLED  changes colors in relation to different tempature ranges which relate to different types (colors) of wax. The attached LED's will distinguish whether or not the tempature is on the high or low end of the range and is this overlapping with the wax above or below the one displayed by the on board RGB.

Project Description:
My project is a temperature sensor that directs users to the appropriate wax based on the temperature. 
The colors that appear on the RGB are coordinated to the wax colors of Swix products.
The two off board LEDs alert users if the temperature is overlapping with the wax above or below the color shown by the on board. 
It is important and interesting to me as it solves a common problem in an exciting way.
I learned how to effectively translate real world data into ranges that guide my code.
Highly useful gadget! 

Biggest Challenge:
So many else if statements, making sure everything was set up in the right ranges to mimic logic of wax tempature ranges and ensure I had perfect syntax to achieve the correct behavior.

Motivation:
It is important and interesting to me as it solves a real problem in an exciting way.

What I Am Most Proud Of:
The simplicity of my design and its effectiveness in communicating information. Taking a real product vision and bringing it to life with code.

3 Tips to My Past Self:
  1. Arrays Are the Best!
  2. Make sure you make the right pin the right color! ie. 12=R, 13=G, 14=B,
  3. Print statements are your best friend, they will help identify all of your silly syntax problems
